Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 31, 2026  ·  Applies to: CG Live (App)  ·  Operated by: Graphicy

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Graphicy ("we," "us," "our") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with CG Live ("the App," "CG Live"), our CRM suite available as a web platform and as an Android application. This policy covers CG Live's use of the Meta Platforms (Facebook, Instagram, Threads, and WhatsApp Business) APIs, and applies whether CG Live is used directly by a business or deployed as a white-label platform on behalf of one of our partners.

2. Information We Collect

To provide CG Live's features, we collect and process the following categories of information when you connect your accounts:

  • Account & profile information — name, email address, and basic profile details from your Facebook, Instagram, Threads, or WhatsApp Business account, obtained via Meta's login and authorization flow.
  • Page and business asset data — Facebook Page details, Instagram professional account details, Threads profile details, and WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) details, including phone numbers and message templates.
  • Content data — posts, captions, and media you create or schedule through CG Live for publishing to Facebook, Instagram, or Threads.
  • Engagement data — comments, replies, and direct messages on your connected Facebook, Instagram, Threads, and WhatsApp accounts, which CG Live retrieves and allows you to respond to.
  • Insights & analytics data — performance metrics such as reach, impressions, engagement, and profile views for your connected accounts and published content.
  • Webhook event data — real-time notifications from Meta (e.g. new messages, comments, or status updates) that CG Live subscribes to in order to power live updates in your dashboard.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information above strictly to operate CG Live's features, including:

  • Scheduling and publishing posts on your behalf across Facebook, Instagram, and Threads.
  • Displaying, and enabling replies to, comments and direct messages across Facebook, Instagram, Threads, and WhatsApp within the CG Live dashboard.
  • Sending and receiving WhatsApp Business messages and managing WhatsApp Business Account settings on your behalf.
  • Generating performance and insights dashboards for your connected accounts.
  • Maintaining the security, reliability, and functioning of the App.
CG Live does not use the data obtained from Meta APIs for advertising purposes, and does not sell user data to third parties.

4. How Information Is Shared

We share information only as necessary to operate the service:

  • With Meta Platforms — to send and receive content, messages, and insights via the Facebook Graph API, Instagram API, WhatsApp Business Platform, and Threads API, as authorized by you.
  • With white-label partners — if you access CG Live through one of our white-label partners, relevant account and usage data may be visible to that partner's administrators as part of the platform they operate for you.
  • With service providers — infrastructure providers (e.g. hosting, storage) who process data solely on our behalf and under confidentiality obligations.
  • For legal reasons — where required to comply with applicable law, regulation, or legal process.

5. Data Storage & Security

Access tokens and connected account credentials are encrypted at rest. We apply industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as your account remains connected to CG Live and as needed to provide the service. If you disconnect a platform account or delete your CG Live account, associated tokens and cached data are deleted in accordance with our Data Deletion Instructions.

7. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You may disconnect any connected platform account at any time from within CG Live's account settings, or request full deletion of your data. See our Data Deletion Instructions page for the full process.

8. Children's Privacy

CG Live is intended for business use and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from individuals under the age of 18.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date at the top of this page.

10. Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy or how CG Live handles your data, contact us at support@graphicy.in.
