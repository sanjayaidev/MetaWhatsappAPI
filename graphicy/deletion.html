Data Deletion Instructions

Effective date: July 31, 2026  ·  Applies to: CG Live (App)  ·  Operated by: Graphicy

1. Overview

CG Live, developed and operated by Graphicy, integrates with Facebook, Instagram, Threads, and WhatsApp Business through Meta's APIs. This page explains how you can request the deletion of your data from CG Live, in accordance with Meta's Platform Data Deletion requirements.

2. What Gets Deleted

When you request data deletion, we remove the following from CG Live's systems:

  • Access tokens and credentials for your connected Facebook, Instagram, Threads, and WhatsApp Business accounts.
  • Cached profile, Page, and business account information retrieved via Meta APIs.
  • Scheduled or draft content stored within CG Live.
  • Cached comments, direct messages, and engagement data retrieved for display in the CG Live dashboard.
  • Cached insights and analytics data associated with your account.
Deleting your data from CG Live does not delete content, messages, or account data stored directly on Facebook, Instagram, Threads, or WhatsApp themselves. Those are governed by Meta's own policies.

3. How to Delete Your Data

You can request data deletion using either of the following methods:

Option A — In-App Deletion

  1. Log in to CG Live and go to Account Settings.
  2. Select Connected Accounts to disconnect individual Facebook, Instagram, Threads, or WhatsApp accounts, or select Delete My Account to remove all data associated with your CG Live account.
  3. Confirm the request. Disconnection of platform tokens is processed immediately; full account data deletion is completed within 30 days.

Option B — Email Request

  1. Send a data deletion request to support@graphicy.in from the email address associated with your CG Live account.
  2. Include your account email and, if applicable, the connected Facebook, Instagram, Threads, or WhatsApp account(s) you'd like removed.
  3. We will confirm receipt and complete the deletion within 30 days, and notify you once finished.

4. Removing CG Live via Meta

You can also remove CG Live's access to your Facebook, Instagram, or Threads account directly from Meta's platforms at any time:

  • Facebook / Instagram / Threads: Go to Meta Account Center → Apps and Websites → CG Live → Remove.
  • WhatsApp Business: Go to Meta Business Suite → Business Settings → Partners, and revoke CG Live's / Graphicy's partner access to your WhatsApp Business Account.

Removing access this way stops CG Live from retrieving further data, and we will delete any previously cached data associated with the disconnected account within 30 days.

5. Contact Us

For any questions about data deletion or to check the status of a request, contact us at support@graphicy.in.
