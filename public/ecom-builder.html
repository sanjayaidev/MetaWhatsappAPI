How the rest of the flow works

These steps happen automatically once a trigger above fires — nothing else to configure. This part of the flow is fixed by design (not a drag-and-drop chain you build); what you configure is which keywords/channels/products kick it off.

Add to cartCustomer taps a product in the catalog → added to their cart automatically.
View CartShown after every "Add to cart" tap, or the customer can say "cart".
CheckoutTapping "Checkout" creates the order and sends a secure payment link using your connected Payment Gateway.
ConfirmationOnce payment is confirmed, the bot automatically messages the customer back — no manual step needed.
