Connecting a Client's WhatsApp Number via Manual WABA ID + System User Token

The full flow for onboarding an external business's WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) when your Meta App does not yet have Advanced Access to business_management and whatsapp_business_manage_events — using Business Manager asset-sharing as the interim path, plus what changes once App Review is approved.

1Add each other as Business PartnersBusiness-level connection — grants no access on its own

Client does this

The client goes into their own Business Manager and adds your Business ID as a partner. This step only tells Meta the two businesses can share assets with each other — it does not hand over access to anything yet.

Business Settings → Accounts / Partners → Add → Add Partner Business
Your Business ID

Find this under Business Info in your own Business Manager settings. Give this exact ID to every new client.

2Share the WABA asset — not the whole businessThe step people skip

Client does this

Adding you as a partner business is not enough. The client must go one step further and explicitly share their WhatsApp Account asset with your business specifically.

Business Settings → Accounts → WhatsApp accounts → select their WABA → Assign partner / Add partner
Do not ask for Admin access

Never ask the client to make you Admin of their whole Business Manager. That's business-wide control (ad accounts, Pages, billing, other WABAs) and most clients will rightly refuse it. Asset-level sharing, scoped to just the one WhatsApp Account, is the correct and minimal request.

Access level to request: Manage WhatsApp Business Account (full control of just this asset).

3Assign your System User to the shared WABAOn your side

You do this

Once the client has shared the asset, their WABA becomes visible inside your own Business Manager as an assignable resource. Go assign your System User to it.

Business Settings → Users → System users → your system user → Assign Assets → WhatsApp Accounts

Grant Full control / Manage so the token you generate next can subscribe webhooks and send messages on this WABA.

4Generate the System User access tokenOn your side

You do this

Generate the token from your system user — not the client's. Because the asset was properly shared in Steps 2–3, this token now carries permission over their WABA too.

Business Settings → System users → your system user → Generate New Token

Required scopes for this flow, at minimum:

  • whatsapp_business_management
  • whatsapp_business_messaging
Generating a new token is safe

Meta does not revoke previously issued system user tokens when you generate a new one. Existing client integrations already stored in the database keep working on their own saved token — nothing needs to be updated for them.

5Run the manual add flow in the appInside your product

You do this

Use the app's existing manual-connect flow with the client's waba_id and the token generated in Step 4.

Under the hood, this calls:

POST /api/wa/manual/verify   // pulls phone numbers under the WABA
POST /api/wa/manual/save     // saves the account + calls subscribed_apps

The save step automatically subscribes your app to the WABA's webhook events:

POST /{waba_id}/subscribed_apps
Authorization: Bearer {your_system_user_token}

6Confirm the webhook subscription actually firedVerification

You do this

Send a test message to the client's number, or wait for an inbound one, and check your server console for the incoming webhook log line.

[webhook] incoming: {
  "waba_id": "<client waba id>",
  "field": "messages",
  "value": { ... }
}
If nothing appears

Check the raw response from subscribed_apps in Step 5 for an error before assuming the whole flow failed silently — the response is currently only exposed as a boolean (webhook_subscribed), so log the full subData object during testing.

Reference — why this workaround is needed

These two permissions require Meta App Review (Advanced Access) to manage a WABA your app has no direct relationship to. Asset sharing (Steps 2–3) sidesteps that requirement because Meta already trusts the relationship at the account level.

Permission Status (example) What it unlocks
whatsapp_business_management Advanced Access Manage numbers, templates, and subscribe webhooks — for WABAs your app can already reach (own account, or asset-shared).
whatsapp_business_messaging Approved Send/receive messages.
business_management Not approved Reach into a separate business's shared assets directly via API, without prior asset delegation.
whatsapp_business_manage_events Not approved Subscribe/manage webhooks on a WABA your app does not own, without prior asset delegation.

App Review notes for next submission

Last rejection reason: the screencast only showed a single-business flow (your own WABA). Reviewers need to see the app managing a second, separate business's WABA to approve cross-business permissions.

Recording script to use next time:

  1. Log in as your own business/admin in the product's dashboard.
  2. Start "Add Number Manually" using a genuine second test business's WABA ID + token.
  3. Show the verify step pulling their phone numbers.
  4. Show save/connect, and call out the moment subscribed_apps fires for that client's WABA.
  5. Show the success/confirmation state.
  6. Ideally, show a live inbound message from that client's number landing in the dashboard.
Submit together

business_management and whatsapp_business_manage_events are functionally paired for this use case — submit both in the same review round, with the same recording, and make sure the written justification explicitly describes a Tech Provider managing WABAs on behalf of separate client businesses.

Quick checklist

  • Client added your Business ID as a partner
  • Client shared their specific WABA asset with your business (not full Admin)
  • Your system user assigned to their shared WABA in your Business Manager
  • New token generated from your own system user
  • Manual add flow run in the app with client's waba_id + your token
  • Console log confirms [webhook] incoming for the client's waba_id
  • Existing/other client integrations left untouched — no token updates needed for them
